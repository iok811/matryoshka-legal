Privacy Policy — Матрёшка Мариса

Last updated: 31 October 2026

This policy explains what data the Матрёшка Мариса app (“the app”) collects, how it is used and who it is shared with. It is a translation of the Italian version; in case of any difference, the Italian version prevails.

1. Data stored by the app

The app stores the following data generated by your use only on your device (never on an app server):

  • Learning progress: completed lessons, streak of consecutive days, level reached
  • Answers to exercises and the related review schedule (for spaced repetition)
  • App preferences: voice settings, language, chosen level
  • Exercise packs generated at your request

This data stays on your device, is never sold and is not shared with third parties for advertising purposes. There are no ads in the app.

2. Subscription and purchases

Levels B1–C2 require a subscription (annual, or a one-time “lifetime” purchase), managed through RevenueCat, which relies on the payment system of your Apple or Google account — the app does not handle your payment card details directly. RevenueCat receives an anonymous identifier linked to your purchase in order to check that the subscription is active; this data is used only to unlock the content, not for advertising purposes. You can read RevenueCat’s privacy policy at revenuecat.com.

3. Third-party services you can choose to turn on

Only if you explicitly turn them on in the Settings, the app integrates the following third-party services:

Native-speaker voice included in the subscription

If you have an active subscription and turn this option on, the text to be spoken is sent from your device to the app’s server, which forwards it to an external speech-synthesis service (ElevenLabs, elevenlabs.io, or Google Cloud Text-to-Speech, cloud.google.com) using a key managed by the app itself — you do not need to provide anything. The text sent is only that of the current exercise, with no personal data. To limit the cost of the service, the server may record, for each user, the monthly voice usage (amount of text synthesised) associated with the anonymous RevenueCat identifier of your purchase; no name, e-mail or other personal data is recorded, nor your progress. Sentences that have already been synthesised may be kept on the server and reused for other users.

Free natural voice (levels A1 and A2)

Even without a subscription, for the ready-made sentences the app can play a natural Russian voice: your device sends the app’s server only the text of the sentence you want to hear (possibly together with an anonymous app identifier), and the server returns the audio it has already stored. In this case the text is not forwarded to any external speech service, and no name, e-mail or other personal data is sent. If the sentence is not available, the app uses your device’s voice.

Premium voice with your own key (alternative)

If you prefer not to subscribe but still want a natural voice, you can enter your own personal ElevenLabs API key: in this case the text to be spoken is sent from your device to the app’s server, which forwards it to ElevenLabs using your key (ElevenLabs cannot be called directly from a browser). The server never saves or records your key — it uses it only for that single request and discards it immediately afterwards. The key does, however, remain saved on your device, in a form readable by anyone with physical access to the device or browser — it is not meant to be a safe place for sensitive secrets. Use of this service is governed by ElevenLabs’ privacy policy, available at elevenlabs.io.

Content generated with artificial intelligence

The generation of new exercise packs (a feature reserved for subscribers) uses a language model (Claude, by Anthropic) through the app’s server, which manages its own key without sharing it with you. Your personal data (name, e-mail, etc. — which the app does not ask for anyway) is never included in these requests: only the text needed to generate the exercise.

The corrections and explanations you request with the dedicated buttons (for example “💡 Why is it wrong?”) also go through the same server and model: only the exercise text and your answer are sent, never personal data.

4. Device permissions

The app may ask for the following permissions, only when they are needed for a feature you are actively using:

  • Microphone: used only when you turn on the pronunciation check, to compare your pronunciation with the target text. The app does not save the audio and does not send it to its own servers. Speech processing is, however, carried out by the speech-recognition service of your device or browser (Apple or Google, depending on the device), which may also perform it on its own servers according to its own privacy policies.
  • Notifications: used for an optional daily reminder with the number of words to review. You can turn them off at any time in your operating system’s settings.

5. Data the app does NOT collect

The app does not collect: location, contacts, photos/videos from your gallery, advertising identifiers (IDFA), browsing history outside the app.

6. Data retention and deletion

Progress data stays saved on your device until you delete it yourself (from the app settings, if a reset function is available) or until you uninstall the app — since there is no server keeping your progress, there is nothing to ask to be deleted on that side. Subscription data (active/expired status) is handled by RevenueCat according to its retention policy. Voice usage records (section 3) are associated only with the anonymous purchase identifier; to ask for their deletion, write to the address given in section 8.

7. Children

The app is not specifically aimed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from this age group in any way other than as described above (no identifying data is requested in any case).
